# 02. Problem Statement

## The Problem
AI in healthcare evolution is heavily fragmented across academic papers, company press releases, and dense industry reports. No single, cohesive visual narrative exists that tracks this transformation in a digestible, engaging way.

## The Impact
Students, researchers, and business professionals lack a quick, interactive reference for understanding AI healthcare milestones. This cognitive overload makes it difficult to grasp the magnitude and speed of AI's integration into medicine.

## The Proposed Solution
The "Evolution of AI in Healthcare (2000–2026)" project delivers a world-class, visually stunning, emotionally immersive digital experience. It acts as an interactive historiography featuring:
- Scroll-reactive cinematic timeline
- "Atmospheric Intelligence" 3D backgrounds
- Oracle.AI embedded medical historian chatbot

## Assumptions Made
*   Users prefer high-fidelity, scroll-driven interactive narratives over static text documents.
*   A "dark, cinematic" aesthetic (similar to a Netflix documentary or Apple keynote) increases engagement.
*   Integrating a live LLM (Oracle.AI) scoped strictly to medical history will provide a unique edge over traditional timelines.
