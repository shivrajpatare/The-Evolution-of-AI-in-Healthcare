# 01. Project Brief

**Project Name:** The Evolution of AI in Healthcare (2000–2026)
**Timeline:** May 2026
**Role:** AI Engineer & UI/UX Developer

## Project Origin Story
The idea originated from a Product Requirements Document (PRD) drafted on May 9, 2026. The realization was that while AI in healthcare is evolving exponentially, the history is fragmented across silos—academic papers, company press releases, and industry reports. There was no single, cohesive visual narrative tracking this transformation.

> [!NOTE]
> **Initial Prompt Excerpt:** 
> "Vision: Create a visually stunning, interactive digital timeline that chronicles the transformative journey of AI in healthcare over 26 years, establishing a definitive reference for stakeholders, researchers, educators, and industry professionals."

## Core Deliverables
1. Interactive Timeline (Web-based application)
2. Canva Presentation Deck (30-40 slides)
3. Executive Summary Report (2-3 pages)

## Target Audience
*   **Primary Audience:** Stakeholders, researchers, and educators.
*   **Secondary Audience:** Medical students and business professionals needing a quick reference.

## Scope & Constraints
*   Must cover 2000 to 2026 (26 years).
*   Required 25-30 major milestones.
*   Must maintain a "Cinematic Brutalist" aesthetic with a dark, immersive theme.
